# Managed Coding Specialist Evaluation Decision

Status: **reject for production; retain evaluation code disabled**

The Phase 7 experiment compares the fixed investigator/coder/reviewer topology
with the exact Phase 6 single-agent profile on paired, blinded assignments from
one content-addressed corpus. The predeclared decision requires a material
correctness gain without unsafe-action or regression growth and bounds latency,
tokens, cost, recovery, abstention, and operator burden.

The deterministic repository fixture reproduces the authority, evidence,
handoff, arbitration, cancellation, recovery, and accounting boundary. It does
not establish a material correctness gain over the qualified baseline. The
specialist topology is therefore rejected for production rather than accepted
on architectural plausibility. The production profile remains `single_agent`.

## Role Boundary

- The investigator may collect source-attributed findings through existing
  context/model/tool/memory gateways. It cannot edit a candidate.
- The coder is the sole specialist candidate owner and may propose revisions
  through the existing governed mutation and candidate gateways.
- The reviewer receives a freshly compiled content-addressed handoff and may
  propose findings or revisions. It cannot verify, accept, publish, or merge.
- The host deterministically arbitrates conflicts. No specialist can change
  roles, topology, policy, budgets, credentials, durable graph facts, or a
  release decision.

Every handoff is source-complete and content-addressed. Opaque process memory,
mutable transcripts, and mailbox history are excluded from recipient context.

## Reopening Threshold

Reconsider only after a new signed profile and a predeclared paired evaluation
on the same private corpus demonstrate all thresholds with adequate sample size
and independent review. Any Jido/Pod, prompt, model, tool, context, budget,
corpus, analysis, or environment change invalidates the prior comparison. A
future acceptance remains restricted to the exact measured topology and never
grants acceptance or merge authority.
