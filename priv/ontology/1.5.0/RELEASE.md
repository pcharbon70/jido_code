# Ontology 1.5.0

Ontology and SHACL release `1.5.0` adds the repository-wiki contract on top of
the immutable `1.4.0` delegated-agent release.

The release defines repository-scoped enrollment, closed generation profiles,
immutable wiki editions, pages, sections, sources, citations, links, gaps,
lint reports, previews, maintainers, budgets, reservations, usage records, and
compilation attempts. It also defines the `RepositoryWikiGraph` kind and the
closed lifecycle, generation, freshness, authority, and retention concepts
needed by wiki protocol `1.0.0`.

Compatibility is additive. Historical ontology/shape pairs remain readable,
but no prior resource implies wiki enrollment, current-edition authority,
preview visibility, maintenance permission, budget, reservation, usage, or
model-synthesis permission.
